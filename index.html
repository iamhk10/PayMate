      ✨ About PayMate

      PayMate is a free, simple web app that helps friends, college students and groups create clean expense bills in PDF and JPG format. Add expenses, choose who paid, split the amount, and download or share your bill—no login, no signup, no hidden charges.

      💌 Developer's Note

      PayMate started with one simple idea: friends should not waste time calculating who paid what and who owes how much. Whether it is a market trip, canteen bill, college trip, or hostel expense, PayMate keeps everything clear. If you find it useful, share it with your friends. Your feedback means a lot to me.

      🔒 Privacy

      PayMate does not require login. Your bills and drafts are saved locally in your browser. We do not collect your personal expense data.
